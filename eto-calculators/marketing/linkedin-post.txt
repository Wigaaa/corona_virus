⚡ Everything an ETO Needs – now online and free

After 12+ years as an Electro-Technical Officer on DP2 offshore vessels and jack-up barges, I kept wishing for one place with the calculations, system knowledge and fault-finding steps we need every day on board.

So I built it: 📘 ETO Handbook – Marine Electrical Engineering, Guides & Tools.

What's inside:
🧮 29 calculator pages, 400+ calculations – cables, generators, motors, protection, harmonics, transformers, batteries & UPS, thrusters, jacking, PMS / blackout simulator… each with the formula used and a clear ✅ / ⚠️ / ⛔ verdict
📘 32 system guides written for the ETO – DP, thrusters, jacking, jetting, raw water, PMS, diesel generators, MSB & ESB, ESD, fire & gas, cranes, pumps, valves, BWMS, chillers and more
🩺 Troubleshooting & preventive maintenance – symptom → causes → checks for every system
📝 192 quiz questions – test your understanding of each system with instant feedback
🦺 Safety & Ex – isolation, LOTO, HV safety and hazardous areas
🎯 Career tools – instruments, meter tricks, survey tips and offshore interview preparation

📱 Opens in any browser on phone, tablet or PC – no app, no sign-up, free. Tip: on your phone tap "Add to Home Screen" to open it like an app. Dark mode included for night watches.

It is built for ETOs, electricians and marine engineers – juniors learning the systems and seniors who want quick answers.
