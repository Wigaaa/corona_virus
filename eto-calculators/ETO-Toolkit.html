📘 Quick tips

  • Every calculator shows the formula used. Green / amber / red tiles flag acceptable, borderline and unacceptable values.
  • Fields marked with a table (load lists, DG sharing, blackout steps) can have rows added or removed.
  • Use 📋 Copy results to paste into a requisition / report, or 🖨 Print for a PDF record.
  • Default values are typical examples (440 V / 60 Hz marine system) – replace them with your vessel's nameplate data.
  • Set your vessel & ETO profile once on the Test Records page – it is printed on every report, checklist and troubleshooting record.
  • Fault log, spares list and checklists are stored only in this browser – use Copy results regularly as a backup.
  • Cable / breaker tables and class rules differ by vessel: the vessel's design documents and class society rules are always the authority.

⚡ About this toolkit

ETO Toolkit is a free marine electrical engineering & troubleshooting toolbox for ETOs: 300+ calculators, test records, troubleshooting guides, checklists and simulators – power, cables, motors, generators, protection, drives, harmonics, jacking, cranes, thrusters, power-plant analysis, plus unit converters, maths, geometry, circuit theory and mechanical / thermal formulas – built for daily work on a jack-up barge. It runs offline in any browser. Calculations follow commonly used IEC / IEEE / IACS practice and are aids only: always verify against the vessel's drawings, equipment nameplates, manufacturer data and class / flag rules. Feedback and suggestions are welcome by email or WhatsApp.
